# Provenance of the manuscript evidence

The repository separates prospective confirmation, independent participant replication, and later frozen falsification analyses.

- **Level-5A**: prospective new-participant confirmation, frozen 20 Aug 2026. Archived `SGIT_Level5A_results.json` and participant output provide the primary FULL/AP/AMP evidence.
- **Level-6A strict template**: second non-overlapping 30-participant cohort, representation transferred without retuning.
- **PAPER01**: frozen reviewer-control package after Level-5/6 outcomes were already known. It tested alternative coordinates/bases, prestimulus geometry, association metrics, and a first amplitude-preserving phase shuffle.
- **PAPER02**: frozen after the surprising PAPER01 phase-shuffle outcome and before PRODUCT/INTERFERENCE/ANGLE outcomes. It decomposed AP and FULL into exact components.
- **PAPER03**: frozen after PAPER02 and before the direct phase-null outcomes. It compared observed INTERFERENCE and ANGLE against 999 amplitude-preserving category-phase null realizations and verified observed scores against archived PAPER02 participant references.

The fact that PAPER01-03 are frozen before their own targeted outcomes does not make them new participant-level confirmations: they reuse the same two cohorts. The manuscript preserves this distinction explicitly.
