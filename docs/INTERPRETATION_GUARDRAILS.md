# Interpretation guardrails

Supported by the archived analyses:

- The frozen full complex early-alpha category geometry is reproducible across independent run ensembles in two non-overlapping N=30 cohorts.
- The exact AP component is more cross-run reproducible than the exact AMP component in both cohorts.
- The AP>AMP ordering is robust to out-of-bag PCA, a rank-matched reconstructed sensor-coordinate representation, 49 random orthogonal rotations of the 16-mode subspace, Pearson/Kendall association metrics, and a far-prestimulus negative control.
- Both PRODUCT and INTERFERENCE geometries are reproducible; ANGLE is reproducible even after removing amplitude weights.
- Observed INTERFERENCE and ANGLE reliabilities exceed an amplitude-preserving category-phase permutation null in both cohorts under the frozen PAPER03 decision rule.

Not established:

- physiological phase-amplitude coupling (PAC);
- direct inter-regional communication-through-coherence;
- causal necessity or sufficiency;
- a universal phase code;
- source-space or whole-sensor-rank generality;
- single-trial invariance;
- awareness/consciousness specificity.
